RADCounter

Real-time RVU & TBWU tracking for the radiology reading room

RADCounter is a lightweight desktop companion that counts your work as you read it — live RVU and TBWU totals, per-hour pace against a goal, projected end-of-shift income, and a full statistics view of your shifts. It identifies each study straight from Mosaic and Clario, compares its own work units against Clario's, and keeps a private local record of every shift. One small exe, no installer, auto-updating.

  • Closing the app can no longer discard the study you just signed while it waits for MosaicTools to confirm it, and studies signed with no shift running now warn before they're lost.
  • An update can no longer pair a new version with your old pay rates: pricing files are staged and installed at next launch, and a failure cancels the update instead of reporting success.
  • End-of-shift reconciliation no longer offers to delete studies that really are in Clario after an incomplete read followed by a failed one.
  • A dollar shift target could paint "est shift total" red while "avg/hour" was green at the same moment; both now use the same rate-aware maths, with an arrow so colour isn't the only cue.
  • Settings, Statistics and Tools no longer open off-screen after a monitor is disconnected, and closing Settings with the X no longer keeps a theme you were only previewing.
