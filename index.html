RADCounter

Real-time RVU & TBWU tracking for the radiology reading room

RADCounter is a lightweight desktop companion that counts your work as you read it — live RVU and TBWU totals, per-hour pace against a goal, projected end-of-shift income, and a full statistics view of your shifts. It identifies each study straight from Mosaic and Clario, compares its own work units against Clario's, and keeps a private local record of every shift. One small exe, no installer, auto-updating.

✨ New in v6.0.0

v6.0.0No pay data in the download
  • RADCounter no longer ships TBWU and associated compensation information. Every number it uses to value your work comes from Clario, as you sign each study, and from your own monthly Matrix payroll sheets.

  • It also learns from the values Clario gives your studies, so a price change reaches RADCounter within days instead of a month later.
  • A procedure you have never read is not guessed. It counts as zero until Clario reports its value, usually within a minute.
  • Clario CDP is highly encouraged: without it, RADCounter cannot learn within the month.
